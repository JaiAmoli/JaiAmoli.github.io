Self-balancing robot

ESP32 · PID control · stepper drive

A two-wheeled robot that stays upright by reading its tilt and correcting dozens of times per second. The simulation at the top of this page uses the same idea.

  • ESP32 microcontroller
  • MPU-6050 IMU for tilt sensing
  • 2× A4988 stepper drivers, 2× NEMA 17 motors with coupler brackets
  • LM2596 buck converter
  • 3S 11.1 V 2200 mAh 35C LiPo

The ESP32 reads the MPU-6050 and runs PID control loops that command the A4988 drivers. Try Kp, Ki and Kd in the simulation: too little Kp and it falls, too much Kd and it jitters.

  • Wire splicing and crimping
  • Setting each driver’s current to match the NEMA 17 rated current
  • Power management: discharge cutoff voltage, wire gauge, thermal limits
