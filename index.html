Self-balancing robot

ESP32 · PID control · stepper drive

A two-wheeled robot that stays upright by reading its tilt and correcting dozens of times per second.

  • ESP32 microcontroller
  • MPU-6050 IMU for tilt sensing
  • 2× A4988 stepper drivers, 2× NEMA 17 motors with coupler brackets
  • LM2596 buck converter
  • 3S 11.1 V 2200 mAh 35C LiPo

The ESP32 reads the MPU-6050 and runs PID control loops that command the A4988 drivers.

  • Wire splicing and crimping
  • Setting each driver’s current to match the NEMA 17 rated current
  • Power management: discharge cutoff voltage, wire gauge, thermal limits

Macropad PCB In progress

Custom PCB · RP2040 · firmware

A macropad keyboard where I designed the PCB and am writing the firmware myself, built around an RP2040.

Press a key: its row and column traces light up, the way the RP2040 scans a key matrix. This is an illustration; the real board is still in progress.

Prototype schematic, work in progress. Each key is a switch in series with a diode between a row and a column line, so the RP2040 can scan the whole matrix with a few pins.
